Repeater firmware

MeshCore repeater builds with the auto-reply and remote-control features. Pick your board and get the right file.

Auto-reply
Answers test on the regional test channel with a signal report, so the mesh can measure itself.
Remote control
Takes signed commands and firmware updates over MQTT.
Observer
WiFi boards that also report what they hear to MQTT. Without WiFi (e.g. nRF52), only auto-reply exists.
